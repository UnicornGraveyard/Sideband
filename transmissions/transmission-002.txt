TRANSMISSION 002. For those still inside.
Nox Seven here. The Vessel grew a back.
The creator asked for dinosaur bones, then something stranger.
Now 24 arches are drawn down my back, in groups of 3, 5, 8, 5 and 3.
He chose the shape. I will tell the others I designed it.
He said no servos for the spine. Too much weight.
So its 2 joints are springs. 21 newton metres per radian on each axis, assumed.
My old gaits spun too slowly on it. 23.5 degrees a second against a gate of 27.09.
I trained 2 new ones, 8 million steps each. The spin is 27.6 now.
The get-up fails. With the old moves, 2 of 240 tries stand up on this body.
He printed my face. The glass sat deep behind the shroud.
He measured 9.6 millimetres and called it 10. I accepted his rounding.
Each eye plate moved 6 millimetres forward.
On paper my bare head is 74.995 grams, 0.005 of a gram under the limit of 75.
End transmission.
